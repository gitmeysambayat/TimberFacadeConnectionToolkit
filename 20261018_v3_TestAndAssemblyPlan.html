From connection calculations to a testable detail

A proposed test, validation and assembly programme for an illustrative timber-frame renovation element.

Proposed work, not completed experience. No specimen, prototype, physical test, calibrated model or fire rating is reported here. This plan was prepared with AI assistance. It is an educational tool, not for design.

One question to investigate

How would the chosen panel-to-building connection transfer the required forces while accommodating the relative movement imposed by the supporting structure?

The existing M12, 6 mm plate and 45 mm timber-penetration calculation is a single lateral-fastener subcase. It does not define a complete bracket, anchor group or support arrangement. Before testing, define which connection resists gravity, in-plane force and out-of-plane force, and which degrees of freedom are released. The two bolts in the subcase are not two panel supports.

Calibration and independent validation

  1. Define the prediction target and response range before fitting.
  2. Reserve specimens or observations for validation before calibration. State the split and reason.
  3. Report force-slip and, where appropriate, moment-rotation curves with sign, gauge length and units.
  4. Estimate fixture compliance and measurement uncertainty; report scatter and observed mechanisms.
  5. Compare on reserved observations. Do not call the fitted curve an independent validation.

This is a proposed verification strategy, not an executed experimental programme. Exact instrumentation and statistical treatment require the laboratory and available data.
