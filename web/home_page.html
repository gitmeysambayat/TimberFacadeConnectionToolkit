An inspectable calculation chain

From assumptions to interpretation

Inputs, equations and limitations are part of the result. The study uses a stated first-generation Eurocode framework and reference material properties.

01 / Define

State the load path

Identify the members, grain direction, fastener, shear planes, action combination and deformation assumptions.

02 / Calculate

Expose the modes

Retain all implemented resistance modes and distinguish characteristic expressions from selected design values.

03 / Compare

Change one condition

Inspect resistance and movement alongside geometry checks. Larger resistance alone does not establish an acceptable detail.

04 / Interpret

Identify the next evidence

Connect the prediction to proposed measurements and keep unmodelled failure modes visible.
